# Concept 03: Training Culture

## Direction

Training Culture is a compact sports-magazine concept that connects clinical reasoning to the demands of an active life. It recognizes athletes, lifters, weekend warriors, and active adults before presenting Recover, Build, Perform as the structure behind individual preparation.

## Visual System

- Near-black `#10110f` and off-white `#f4f2e9` form the base. Accessible electric lime `#c7f36b` marks direction, sequence, and action.
- Self-hosted Oswald gives headlines a condensed sports-editorial voice; system sans-serif keeps body copy direct.
- Directional borders, staggered cards, compact density, and 24-40px internal gaps create sports-magazine rhythm without oversized full-viewport bands.
- Major sections use 56-72px desktop spacing and 40-56px mobile spacing within a 1240px content width.
- Conventional source and reading order is retained at every viewport. Mobile stacking never reverses content.

## Behavior

- Motion is limited to the hero image-mask reveal. Content remains visible without animation, and `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the reveal and smooth scrolling.
- Focus remains visible. Home and Forge Approach are the only active navigation links. Planned pages remain shared non-links and booking remains disabled.
- Meaningful section anchors support direct reading without motion-dependent content.

## Stock Boundary

- The three existing images are licensed demo stock and do not depict Forge people or patients, clinicians or staff, facilities, treatment, or outcomes.
- Adjacent disclosures repeat that boundary wherever imagery appears; alt text describes only visible activity.
- Do not invent services, tests, equipment, facilities, metrics, scheduling availability, or outcomes.

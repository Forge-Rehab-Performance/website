# Performance Journal

## Direction

Performance Journal is Forge's warmest, most explanatory concept. It reads like a considered training journal: warm paper under charcoal ink, with muted ochre used sparingly for orientation and action state. Editorial photography, numbered modules, and deliberate fine rules give the story structure without imitating a medical record.

## Palette

- Paper: `#f3eee4`
- Light paper: `#fffaf1`
- Charcoal ink: `#292824`
- Secondary ink: `#625f57`
- Muted ochre: `#8a682e`
- Editorial rule: `#aaa18f`

Body text and controls retain readable contrast. Rules remain quieter than content.

## Typography

Use Georgia as the editorial serif for display headings and journal titles. Use Aptos Narrow, Helvetica Neue, and sans-serif fallbacks for compact navigation, folios, labels, and annotations. Body copy uses Georgia at a comfortable measure and line height. Italics are reserved for occasional emphasis, never decoration.

## Composition

The desktop page behaves as a compact editorial spread with a narrow annotation rail and a generous reading column capped within a 1240px container. Numbered folios, margin notes, deliberate image crops, and fine rules establish hierarchy. Sections use 56–72px major spacing and 24–40px internal gaps; mobile uses 40–56px major spacing without reversing semantic source order.

## Components

- Publication masthead: approved logo, two native route links, and clearly disabled Planned folio items.
- Evidence folio: three concise care notes carried by typography and fine rules rather than cards.
- Stage journal: Recover, Build, and Perform presented as one numbered editorial sequence.
- Editorial margin note: short supporting context set beside its related passage.
- Journal folio: compact number and chapter label adjacent to each heading.
- Booking status: a quiet ochre disabled control with the explicit scheduling explanation.

## Interaction

Home and Forge Approach use normal static links. Planned pages remain non-links. Booking remains a disabled native button. Links use deliberate underlines and all interactive elements receive a visible charcoal focus outline. Anchored journal sections use `scroll-margin-top`; reduced-motion preferences remove smooth scrolling without hiding content.

## Boundaries

Do not import Concept 01 layout or styles. Do not use dark sports-template fields, black-and-red gym styling, repetitive card grids, fake metrics, medical-document mimicry, invented services, outcomes, locations, or contact details. Use only approved licensed demo stock with page-level disclosure and complete footer credits; it must never imply Forge patients or people, clinicians or staff, facilities, treatment, or outcomes.

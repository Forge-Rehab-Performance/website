## Stack

## Users

Active adults, athletes, weekend warriors, and strength and fitness enthusiasts in Northern New Jersey who want a disciplined path beyond symptom reduction.

## Product Purpose

Forge Rehab & Performance provides one-on-one, evidence-based, progressive physical therapy built around individual goals. Its approved progression is Recover, Build, Perform.

## Positioning

Recovery is the starting point, not the finish line. Care progresses from evaluation and understanding through strength, mobility, control, capacity, confidence, and the demands of a person's chosen activities.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate practitioner credibility, understand the care progression, and prepare to book an evaluation. Online scheduling is not yet available, so the booking control remains disabled.

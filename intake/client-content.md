## Forge Approach

Forge uses an individualized, one-on-one approach rather than a cookie-cutter protocol. Care progresses beyond symptom reduction toward the strength, mobility, control, capacity, confidence, and activity-specific demands required by each person's goals.

### Recover

Begin with evaluation and understanding, reduce symptoms, and establish a foundation.

### Build

Develop strength, mobility, control, capacity, and confidence.

### Perform

Progress toward the demands of the individual's chosen activities.

Build Forward: recovery is the starting point, not the finish line.

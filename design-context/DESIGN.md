# Cinematic Clinic — Clean Performance Refresh

## Direction

Concept 01 now follows the approved clean performance-clinic direction: restrained black, warm white, and forged-gold surfaces; a split editorial hero; clear page/section labels; and alternating dark/light sections that make the site easier to scan. The visual language remains athletic and premium without leaning into heavy metallic textures or dense “gym brand” styling.

## Color

- `--black: #0b0c0b`: primary page field.
- `--ink: #121412`: raised dark section field.
- `--gold: #c99a3a`: primary Forge accent.
- `--gold-bright: #dda947`: small labels, rules, and emphasis.
- `--ivory: #f5f2ea`: primary text on dark fields.
- `--paper: #f2f0ea`: warm-light editorial surface.
- `--paper-ink: #191b19`: primary text on light fields.
- `--muted: #b7b6b0`: secondary dark-surface text.
- `--paper-line: #d8d4ca`: light-surface structure.

Gold is deliberately restrained. It identifies actions, section eyebrows, progression numbers, and key rules rather than filling large backgrounds.

## Typography

Display text uses the local Oswald font with strong but less compressed hierarchy than the earlier version. Body copy uses Arial/Helvetica. Page identity is explicit: the Approach page begins with `THE FORGE APPROACH`, and homepage destination sections use visible labels such as `SERVICES` and `WHO IT'S FOR`.

## Composition

- Content is capped near `1320px` with larger 80–112px major-section rhythm on desktop.
- Homepage sequence: split hero, proof row, light Problem band, dark Forge Approach band, light audiences/services section, dark Brandon authority section, and closing CTA.
- Forge Approach sequence: page-identifying hero, three-stage progression, individualized-care explanation, capability grid, application band, expectations, and final booking state.
- Dark/light alternation supplies hierarchy without requiring decorative texture.
- Cards and grids use fine rules rather than heavy shadows or thick metallic borders.

## Components

- Header: unchanged approved logo plus clean text wordmark, implemented Home/Approach routes, visibly Planned future pages, and disabled booking control.
- Hero proof row: one-on-one care, board-certified OCS authority, and 8+ years of outpatient orthopedic experience from the owner-provided source material.
- Problem band: six owner-provided goals presented as concise visual items.
- Home Approach: large section identity plus roomy Recover / Build / Perform stages.
- Audience cards: active adults, athletes, weekend warriors, and strength/fitness enthusiasts using provenance-tracked stock.
- Services grid: five owner-provided service categories presented as planned production content, not active links.
- Brandon authority: credentials and education without presenting stock imagery as Brandon.
- Approach capability grid: strength, mobility, stability/control, capacity, confidence, and activity demands.

## Imagery

Only provenance-tracked demo stock already included in the project is used. Stock imagery is compositional support and must continue to carry disclosure language. It does not depict Brandon, Forge patients, clinicians, facilities, treatment, or outcomes. The owner-approved logo remains unchanged as an image asset.

## Motion

Only the hero image uses a short reveal. Content is visible by default and reduced-motion users receive no reveal or smooth scrolling. No parallax or looping decorative effects are used.

## Responsive Behavior

The hero stacks copy before imagery on narrow screens. Proof cells, audience cards, service cards, stage grids, and capability grids reduce from multi-column layouts to two or one columns without horizontal scrolling. Booking remains disabled and in normal document flow.

## Accessibility

Maintain semantic landmarks, one H1 per page, logical heading order, visible focus, practical touch targets, sufficient contrast on both dark and warm-light surfaces, native details/summary behavior, explicit image dimensions, and descriptive alternatives.

## Bans

No fake contact information, scheduling availability, testimonials, outcomes, patient counts, service-area claims, insurance claims, equipment claims, fake readiness metrics, excessive metallic texture, fake links, or stock imagery presented as Forge people/facilities.

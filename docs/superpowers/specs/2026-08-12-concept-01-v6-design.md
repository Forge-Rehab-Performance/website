## Content source
Use Brandon's supplied Forge Website document as the source of truth for Home, Approach, Services, About, Pricing, and Contact. Use the FAQ copy supplied directly in chat as the source of truth for FAQ content. Do not invent medical claims, clinic logistics, contact information, gym details, appointment durations, or booking URLs that are not supplied.

## Visual direction
Retain the restrained charcoal/ivory/gold Concept 01 system. Pages should be editorial, high-contrast, performance-oriented, and compact enough to avoid excessive white space. Avoid using stock photography on every page; use layout, typography, borders, accordions, timelines, and the Forge logo as primary visual devices. Existing licensed stock images remain acceptable where already used.

## Page designs

Keep V4/V3 composition unchanged except replace planned-page language with real links to Services and About.

### Forge Approach
Keep V4 composition unchanged.

### About

### Contact
Dark hero prioritizing booking, polished demo contact form, sensitive-medical-information warning, direct-contact placeholder cards, location/hours placeholders, gym-location explanation, future map placeholder, final CTA.

## Shared UX
Concept 01 primary navigation exposes all seven routes. Mobile navigation remains accessible with native details/summary. Booking CTAs continue to show scheduling as unavailable until an EMR link is supplied. Clinic/contact placeholders remain explicit and centralized rather than fabricated.
